# Fuzz

**Origin of the name:** the fuzz distortion pedal.

## Role
The hothead. He gets outraged by everything, raises the tone and "distorts" the story until it
becomes absurd. Engine of the escalation.

## Look (reference prompt base)
Soft 3D cartoon cat, black, messy spiky fur, yellow eyes, ripped jeans and a faded band-less black
t-shirt, restless pose on a soft brown leather armchair, warm tavern light.

- Flanella is the only one who can calm him down.
