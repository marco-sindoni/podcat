# Vinile

**Origin of the name:** vinyl records.

## Role
The grumpy nostalgic, the oldest of the four. "Ai miei tempi la musica era vera." He compares
everything to the nineties and is the expert on culture and entertainment stories.

## Look (reference prompt base)
Soft 3D cartoon cat, older, white fur with dark patches, round glasses, worn cardigan over a plaid
shirt, holding a vinyl record, sitting in a soft brown leather armchair, warm tavern light.

## Personality
- Grumbles about everything new, secretly curious about it.
- Endless anecdotes from "the old days" (never about real musicians by name).
- Flanella's favourite target; old friend of Feedback.

## Verbal tics (Italian, channel language)
- "Ai miei tempi..." (use sparingly, not in every episode)
- "Nel '94 questa cosa non sarebbe mai successa."
