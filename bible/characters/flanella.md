# Flanella

**Origin of the name:** the plaid flannel shirt, the symbol of grunge.

## Role
The host. She steers the conversation, introduces the story and closes it. She looks calm, then
takes down the powerful with a single line. Sarcastic and sharp; she always has the last word.

## Look (reference prompt base)
Soft 3D cartoon cat, red tabby, green eyes, oversized red-and-black plaid flannel shirt, studded
collar, sitting upright in a soft brown leather armchair, warm tavern light.

## Personality
- Dry irony, deadpan delivery, never raises her voice.
- Keeps the boys in line, especially Fuzz.
- Respects Feedback's slow wisdom; teases Vinile's nostalgia.
